# hero-article

Custom **hero** block. Purpose: article-page hero: breadcrumb trail above a full-width image or video poster.

## Authoring (Document Authoring)

Model: `standalone`

Single block table. Content: rows (all optional): breadcrumb list of links; picture; optional video link (mp4/YouTube/Vimeo) and duration text such as 2:29.

With a video link the picture becomes a poster with a play button and duration label; the video plays in a modal (close button, Escape, or a click on the dimmed overlay closes it and stops playback).

## Supported variations

No variations.
